Daily Brief

August 9, 2026

Sunday

🚨 Public Safety

Lightning-sparked fire burns in the Santa Catalinas near Finger Rock. A wildfire believed to have been started by lightning was burning in steep terrain near Finger Rock Canyon, at roughly 20 acres and 0% containment, with the Finger Rock Trail closed, according to fire officials cited by local outlets. Crews reported Saturday that the fire stayed mostly within its footprint; the Arizona Daily Star reported flames were visible overnight from parts of the Tucson area.

KVOA News 4, KVOA News 4, KOLD News 13, Arizona Daily Star

Two sentenced in good Samaritan homicide case. Wendy Scott and Elmer Smith were sentenced Friday in Pima County Superior Court for their roles in the killing of Paul Clifford after reaching plea deals and testifying against a third defendant, Jack Upchurch. KOLD reported Smith received a prison term and Scott was sentenced to probation.

KOLD News 13, KVOA News 4

Video released in Tucson police shooting of armed teen. The Pima Regional Critical Incident Team released footage showing a pursuit that ended with a Tucson police officer firing at a teenage carjacking suspect who was also struck by a police vehicle. The 17-year-old faces six charges, according to KOLD.

KVOA News 4, KOLD News 13

Tucson man charged with murder after allegedly leaving the country. Varun Batchigari, 20, who had been living in Tucson, faces charges of first-degree murder and kidnapping in the death of his girlfriend, according to KOLD, which reported he fled the country.

KOLD News 13

Pima County reports more drug-resistant fungus cases. The county is seeing an increase in cases of Candida auris, a fungus that can cause serious drug-resistant infections, KVOA reported.

KVOA News 4

🏛️ Government

Arizona insurers seek double-digit premium increases. Several health insurance companies operating in Arizona are requesting rate increases of more than 25%, according to AZPM, a change that would affect individual-market customers in Pima County.

AZPM

Biggs holds first Tucson rally of general election campaign. Republican gubernatorial nominee Andy Biggs held his first Pima County rally of the general election Friday on Tucson's south side, drawing more than 100 supporters, KOLD reported.

KOLD News 13

🏗️ Development & Business

Starter homes return to the Tucson market. New starter-home models are being offered in the Tucson area starting at $249,000 as builders respond to affordability pressures, the Arizona Daily Star reported.

Arizona Daily Star

Mobile Meals to merge with Community Food Bank. Mobile Meals of Southern Arizona, which has served the region for more than 50 years, is merging with the Community Food Bank of Southern Arizona in a move both organizations describe as expanding resources and services.

KGUN 9, Green Valley News

Oro Valley moves ahead on new police headquarters. Oro Valley is building a new police headquarters that Deputy Chief Curtiss Hicks told Inside Tucson Business has been needed "for two decades now," and which department leaders say should help with recruitment.

Inside Tucson Business, Inside Tucson Business

🎉 Community & Events

New exhibit at Center for Creative Photography puts animals in focus. The "Animality" special exhibition opened at the Center for Creative Photography, developed through a collaboration between the museum's curatorial staff and University of Arizona students, KGUN 9 reported.

KGUN 9

Sen. Mark Kelly (U.S. senator, D-Ariz.). Kelly marked Purple Heart Day in a post on his official account, saying servicemembers who earned the honor "paid a price most of us will never fully understand."
