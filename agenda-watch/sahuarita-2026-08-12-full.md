Notice of Public Meeting
Pursuant to ARS §38-431.02, notice is hereby given to the members of the Sahuarita Unified School District Governing Board and the public that the Sahuarita Unified School District will hold a Governing Board meeting. The meeting will be held in-person at
Sahuarita District Auditorium, 350 W. Sahuarita Rd. Building #21, Pod B, Sahuarita, AZ 85629.
The meeting will also be live streamed on YouTube and recorded for the public. Below is the information that is needed to view the meeting virtually or listen to it by phone. Information will also be posted on our website –
www.susd30.us
, agenda can also be accessed through the Diligent website
https://susd-30.community.highbond.com/Portal/
.
The agenda is subject to change, up to 24 hours prior to the scheduled Board Meeting. Any changes will be noted on the revised agenda that is posted 24 hours prior to the scheduled meeting.
The Sahuarita Unified School District would like to ensure the accessibility of these meetings to all community members, persons with disabilities may request reasonable accommodations by contacting Betsy Palacios, Executive Assistant to the Superintendent/Governing Board, at bpalacios@sahuarita.net. Please submit your request two weeks prior to the scheduled meeting to allow time for arrangements.
